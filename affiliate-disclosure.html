Rajnish Store participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means that when you click on certain links on our website and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. This disclosure is provided in accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and applicable international regulations.

🔑 The Quick Version

Some links on Rajnish Store are affiliate links. If you click and buy, we earn a small commission. This never increases the price you pay. We only recommend products we genuinely believe in, regardless of whether we earn a commission.

01 What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based marketing model where a business rewards partners (affiliates) for referring customers that result in a sale or other desired action. Affiliates receive a commission for directing traffic and customers to the business.

As an online retailer with an affiliate program, Rajnish Store both:

  • Operates as a merchant: We work with content creators, bloggers, and influencers who promote our products and earn commissions on referred sales.
  • Participates as an affiliate: In some cases, links on our website may direct you to third-party platforms (such as Amazon or manufacturer websites) where we earn referral fees if you make a purchase.

02 Our Affiliate Partnerships

Rajnish Store maintains affiliate relationships with a variety of platforms and brands. Our current primary affiliate partners include:

  • Amazon Associates Program — We may link to products on Amazon.com and earn qualifying referral fees.
  • ShareASale Network — We work with multiple merchants through the ShareASale affiliate network.
  • Commission Junction (CJ Affiliate) — Additional brand partnerships facilitated through CJ Affiliate.
  • Direct Brand Partnerships — Some brands we feature have direct affiliate arrangements with us.
ℹ️ Affiliate links are clearly identifiable — they typically contain tracking parameters. However, the content and quality of our recommendations are never influenced by affiliate relationships.

03 How We Earn Commissions

When you click an affiliate link on Rajnish Store, a tracking cookie is placed in your browser. If you complete a qualifying purchase within the cookie window, we receive a commission from the merchant or affiliate network. The process works as follows:

  • You click an affiliate link on our site.
  • A tracking cookie is stored in your browser (typically 30–90 days).
  • You make a qualifying purchase on the merchant's site.
  • The merchant's affiliate system records the referral.
  • We receive a commission, usually 30–60 days after the sale is confirmed and the return window has passed.

Important: You pay the exact same price whether or not you use our affiliate link. Commissions are paid by the merchant, not by you.

05 Our Editorial Independence

Our commitment to you is unwavering: affiliate relationships never influence our product recommendations, reviews, or editorial content.

  • Products are selected based on quality, value, and customer satisfaction — not commission rates.
  • We regularly feature and recommend products from merchants where we have no affiliate relationship.
  • Our editorial team is separated from our partnerships team to prevent conflicts of interest.
  • Products with known quality issues are removed from our store regardless of affiliate status.
  • We disclose any material relationship between Rajnish Store and the brands we feature.

🌟 Our Promise

Every product in our store has been evaluated by our team. We believe in what we sell. If we wouldn't recommend it to a close friend, it doesn't appear on Rajnish Store. Your trust is more valuable to us than any commission.

06 FTC Compliance

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255), we are required to disclose any material connections between Rajnish Store and the brands or products we feature or link to.

This disclosure satisfies that requirement. Additionally:

  • Affiliate links on our site are marked with "(affiliate link)" or similar notation where required by specific platform rules.
  • Product review pages and blog posts that include affiliate links contain a disclosure notice at the top of the page.
  • Social media posts and advertisements that include affiliate links are tagged with #ad, #sponsored, or #affiliate as appropriate.
  • We maintain accurate records of all affiliate relationships as required by applicable law.
ℹ️ For international visitors: We also comply with the UK ASA/CAP guidelines, EU Directive 2005/29/EC on unfair commercial practices, and equivalent regulations in jurisdictions where we operate.

07 Pricing Transparency

We strive to display accurate, up-to-date pricing on all product listings. However, please be aware:

  • Prices may change between the time you view a product and the time you complete your purchase.
  • Sale prices are time-limited and subject to availability.
  • Prices on third-party affiliate sites (e.g., Amazon) are set by those merchants and may differ from prices shown on Rajnish Store.
  • Shipping costs, taxes, and import duties (for international orders) are calculated at checkout and are not included in displayed prices.
