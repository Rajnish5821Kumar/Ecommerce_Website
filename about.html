🌟 Our Story

Built for Dreamers

Rajnish was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to premium products without compromise.

🎯 Mission

We Curate So You Don't Have To

Founded in 2019, Rajnish Store started in a small apartment with a laptop and a dream. Today we serve over 12,000 customers across 40+ countries, offering hand-tested products that actually live up to their promises.

Our team of 48 product specialists scours the globe to find technology and lifestyle products that combine innovation, quality, and value. We never list a product we wouldn't use ourselves.

📅 Journey

From Idea to Global Brand

2019

The Founding

Rajnish Store launches with 12 products and a simple promise: quality over quantity.

2020

First 1,000 Customers

During the pandemic we pivoted to WFH tech, reaching our 1,000-customer milestone.

2021

Global Expansion

Launched international shipping to 20 countries. Revenue crossed $1M for the first time.

2023

Rajnish Rewards Launched

Our loyalty program goes live — over 8,000 members in the first 90 days.

2025

12,000+ Happy Customers

Now in 40+ countries, with 850+ products and a 99% satisfaction rate. The journey continues.
